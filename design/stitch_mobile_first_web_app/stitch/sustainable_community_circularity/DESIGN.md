## Brand & Style

This design system expresses sustainable living through warm, tactile, and community-centric simplicity. Inspired by natural fibers, circular fashion, and neighborhood solidarity, the interface avoids sterile eco-minimalism in favor of an inviting, human-first aesthetic.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Approachable & Warm:** Conversational, comforting, and clear. It removes the guilt from sustainable choices and replaces it with empowerment and direct social connection.
- **Conscious & Natural:** Grounded in earth-derived palettes, unbleached cotton-tinted backgrounds, and organic botanic greens.
- **Systematic & Trustworthy:** Reliable logistical clarity (routes, pickup intervals, recipient transparency) rendered through clean containers and structured information hierarchy.

### Design Movement: Warm Tactile Modernism
A synthesis of modern mobile UI hygiene with warm organic finishes:
- Elevated flat surfaces built from warm off-whites rather than synthetic cool grays.
- Low-contrast, organic border outlines mimicking fabric hems and paper labels.
- Soft, pill-form affordances and spacious touch zones optimized for rapid thumb actions.

## Colors

The palette balances botanical forest greens with comforting terracotta accents and soft unbleached canvas neutrals.

### Palette Roles & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#3D6A4B` / `#4A7C59`):** Represents growth, renewal, and positive action. Applied to primary CTAs, active tab selections, verified organization badges, and impact counters.
- **Secondary (`#8D5B4C`):** Terracotta / warm clay. Applied to secondary narrative highlights, urgent notices, donation category icons, and secondary tags.
- **Tertiary (`#C48B71`):** Soft sand-terracotta. Used for subtle category chips, warm pill containers, highlight badge backdrops, and active interactive borders.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E2923`):** Dark slate-pine. High legibility without the harsh contrast of pitch black, reducing visual fatigue across content-heavy list feeds.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#F9F9F6` (light warm ecru).
  - Card & sheet surface: `#FFFFFF` with muted warmth.
  - Surface subtle/nested: `#F4F5F0` (light sage-tinted neutral for grouped list sections, pill backgrounds, and search inputs).

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides rounded terminals, high x-height, and open counters. This delivers the essential friendly tone required for communal action, while keeping technical donation details legible at small viewport widths.

### Type Rules
- **Display & Section Headers:** Set in bold weights (`700`) with modest negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create clean, human headlines without harsh structural rigidity.
- **Body & Captions:** Kept at regular weights (`400`) on slate gray (`#1E2923`), maintaining crisp legibility against `#F9F9F6` and `#FFFFFF` cards.
- **Numeric Figures & Impact Metric Data:** Rendered with semi-bold to bold weights (`600` - `700`) to celebrate recycling volume and community impact counters.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict **8pt base grid system** (with half-step 4px micro increments) drives all component padding, gaps, and margins.

### Mobile-First Layout Model
- **Container Strategy:** The primary viewport is targeted for mobile devices (360px–430px wide). On larger viewports (tablet and desktop), content constrains inside a centered max-width mobile stage (`max-width-app: 480px`) or transitions into a structured dual-column dashboard.
- **Horizontal Screen Margins:** Fixed at `1rem` (16px) on mobile views, ensuring maximum tap target footprint for lists and action cards while maintaining clean gutter spacing.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 
  - `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) separation between consecutive list items and actionable cards.
  - `1.5rem` (24px) between grouped semantic sections (e.g., hero banner to donation options to nearby dropoff map).
  - Bottom navigation bar offset requires `4.5rem` (72px) bottom padding on scrollable root containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** combined with **tonal layer borders**. This avoids artificial digital elevation in favor of subtle paper-and-cloth layering.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Canvas (Level 0):** `#F9F9F6`. Base application background for scrollable screens.
2. **Surface Low (Level 1):** `#F4F5F0`. Grouped search filters, non-interactive stat containers, and pill badges.
3. **Card & Sheet (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF`. Clickable donation channels, charity organization cards, and modals.
4. **Floating Action & Nav (Level 3):** `#FFFFFF` with ambient shadow, housing bottom navigation bars and sticky action bars.

### Shadow Profiles
- **Subtle Card Shadow:** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Card Border:** `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.07)`. Creates crisp edge definition on OLED screens without harsh lines.
- **Elevated Float Shadow (Bottom Nav, Sheets):** `0 -4px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape geometry, infusing elements with an organic, gentle feel.

### Geometric Application
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Notification Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Cards, Modals, and Action Rows:** `1rem` (16px) outer radius, with internal nested assets (maps, photos) using `0.75rem` (12px) to maintain harmonious concentric curvature.
- **Interactive Badges, Filter Chips, and Primary Action Buttons:** Complete capsule pills (`9999px`), reinforcing touch readiness and tactile comfort.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action Button:** Full-width or inline pill (`9999px`), background `#3D6A4B`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Includes subtle active state press scale (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with border `1.5px solid #3D6A4B`, text `#3D6A4B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, text `#3D6A4B` or `#8D5B4C`, underline on hover, for supplementary actions like "Conocé más sobre nuestro impacto".

### 2. Cards & Selection Rows
- **Action Navigation Card (e.g., "¿Cómo querés donar?"):** `#FFFFFF` surface, `16px` border-radius, `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.07)` border, subtle ambient card shadow. Left icon anchored in `#F4F5F0` circular or soft-square container, title in `headline-sm`, caption in `body-sm`, right-aligned neutral chevron.
- **Organization List Card:** White surface containing avatar/logo on left, organization name, categorized distance badge ("A 1,2 km"), impact tags, and an outline heart favorite icon on the right edge.

### 3. Filter Chips & Badges
- **Status / Filter Pill:** Height 36px, fully rounded capsule (`9999px`), horizontal padding `14px`. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F5F0` background with neutral dark text. Selected: `#3D6A4B` background with white text, or `#8D5B4C` for thematic categories.
- **Distance / Info Tag:** Soft tinted background (`rgba(61, 106, 75, 0.1)`), text `#3D6A4B`, `label-sm`, padding `4px 8px`, rounded `6px`.

### 4. Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Container background `#F4F5F0` with border `1px solid transparent`, focusing to `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #3D6A4B`. Left search icon in `#1E2923` at 40% opacity, placeholder text in `body-md`. Rounded `9999px` (pill) for global map/list searches.

### 5. Interactive Map Cards
- Contained mobile map previews nested inside white parent cards with `12px` border radius. 
- Map pins: `#3D6A4B` teardrop pins with embedded heart or hanger glyphs, complemented by active selected organization flyout overlay at the card's bottom edge.

### 6. Impact Counters & Badges
- Three-column modular stats container on `#FFFFFF` with `#F4F5F0` separator lines.
- Numeric counters rendered in `headline-lg`, accompanied by clean line icons (garments saved, beneficiaries reached, CO2 avoided) in primary green and terracotta accents.

### 7. Bottom Navigation Bar
- Fixed mobile navigation anchored to viewport bottom with blurred white backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- 5 key action tabs (Inicio, Donar, Organizaciones, Empresas, Mi perfil), active tab indicated with `#3D6A4B` filled iconography and active label tint.
